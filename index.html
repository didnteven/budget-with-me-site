Common questions

Where is my budget stored?

On your phone. Accounts, balances and transactions stay in the app's database on your device; we never receive them. Uninstalling the app removes them.

What happens to statements I upload?

The file is sent securely to our server, which uses Google's Gemini AI to read the transactions and sends them back to your phone for review. We don't keep the file.

What do my circles see?

Only your username, your monthly savings rate and your spending split by category, as percentages, and only while sharing is turned on. Never balances, amounts or transactions.

How do I join a friend's circle?

Ask them for their circle's 8-character invite code, then go to Circles → menu → Join with code.

How do I stop sharing or delete my data?

Turn off "Share my savings rate with my circles" to delete everything you've shared. To remove your username, memberships and challenges from our servers, use Circles → menu → Delete my circle data. To clear the budget on your phone, use Upload → Reset local data.

An upload failed. What can I do?

Use a clear PDF export from your bank, or a sharp photo of a receipt (PDF, PNG or JPG, under 8 MB). Each person can upload up to 30 documents a day. You can always add transactions by hand under Accounts → Add.
