Delete your account and data

Budget With Me, published by Step Up Apps

In the app (instant)

  1. Open Circles.
  2. Tap the menu (⋯) in the top corner.
  3. Tap Delete my circle data and confirm.

This permanently deletes your account from our servers immediately, including your username, circle memberships, shared savings rates and category percentages, challenges you created, and feed posts.

To also clear the budget stored on your phone (accounts, transactions, bills), go to Upload → Reset local data, or uninstall the app. That data is never stored on our servers.

By email

If you no longer have the app, email stepupappsnz@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Budget With Me data" and your circle username. We delete the account and all associated data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What we keep

Nothing tied to you. Our hosting providers may keep short-lived operational logs, which expire under their own retention policies.
